# BUS4005 – AA Aviation AI Prompt Engineering Portfolio

**Assignment 1: AI Prompt Engineering Portfolio**

**Business Case:** AA Aviation Sdn Bhd  
**Selected Workflow:** Flight Booking Request to Ticket Issuance

---

## 1. Project Introduction

This project focuses on developing an AI prompt engineering portfolio for AA Aviation Sdn Bhd, using the flight booking process as the selected business workflow.

The project explores how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can support routine administrative activities, improve information processing, reduce repetitive manual tasks, and enhance operational efficiency in the aviation industry.

The portfolio contains ten structured AI prompts designed to support the complete flight booking process, starting from the initial passenger booking request and ending with electronic ticket delivery and booking closure.

## 2. AA Aviation Sdn Bhd – Company Background

AA Aviation Sdn Bhd is the selected business case for this AI prompt engineering project.

For the purpose of this assignment, the company is considered an aviation service provider that assists customers with flight reservations, passenger information management, flight selection, fare quotations, booking coordination, and ticket issuance.

The proposed AI workflow aims to support the company's booking operations by introducing structured AI-assisted processes.

## 3. Business Problem

Flight booking involves several interconnected administrative activities that require accurate information, communication, and coordination.

Traditional manual processes may create several operational challenges:

- Repetitive entry of passenger information.
- Errors or inconsistencies in passenger details.
- Time-consuming flight availability searches.
- Difficulties comparing airline fares.
- Delays in communicating quotations to passengers.
- Manual booking verification and documentation.
- Errors during ticket issuance and delivery.

These challenges can affect operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Therefore, an AI-assisted workflow could help streamline administrative activities while maintaining appropriate human supervision.

## 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To identify opportunities for AI integration in the flight booking process.
2. To develop structured and effective AI prompts for ten workflow stages.
3. To improve the consistency and accuracy of passenger information processing.
4. To support flight availability checking and fare comparison.
5. To improve booking verification and ticket issuance procedures.
6. To reduce repetitive administrative work.
7. To maintain human oversight for important booking and ticketing decisions.

## 5. Proposed AI Solution

The proposed solution uses AI prompt engineering to support different stages of the flight booking workflow.

Each prompt is designed with a specific role, purpose, input requirements, processing tasks, expected output, and restrictions.

The prompts guide the AI assistant to perform structured tasks while reducing the possibility of unsupported assumptions or inaccurate information.

The proposed solution includes:

- Passenger information extraction and validation.
- Flight availability checking.
- Fare quotation and comparison.
- Passenger quotation and approval.
- Flight reservation creation.
- Booking verification.
- Electronic ticket issuance.
- Ticket delivery and booking closure.

AI is intended to assist employees rather than replace authorised personnel or airline booking systems.

## 6. Ten-Stage Flight Booking Workflow

The project contains ten individual prompt documents stored in the `prompts` directory.

| No. | Prompt File | Workflow Stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | P01.md | Booking Request Intake |
| 2 | P02.md | Passenger Detail Extraction |
| 3 | P03.md | Passenger Information Validation |
| 4 | P04.md | Flight Availability Checking |
| 5 | P05.md | Fare Quotation and Comparison |
| 6 | P06.md | Passenger Quotation and Approval |
| 7 | P07.md | Flight Booking and Reservation Creation |
| 8 | P08.md | Booking Verification and Quality Assurance |
| 9 | P09.md | Flight Ticket Issuance |
| 10 | P10.md | Ticket Delivery and Booking Closure |

### Workflow Explanation

**Stage 1–3: Passenger Information Management**

The process begins with receiving a booking request, extracting relevant passenger details, and validating the information before proceeding.

**Stage 4–5: Flight Search and Fare Quotation**

The AI assistant supports flight availability checking and prepares fare comparisons based on authorised and current information sources.

**Stage 6–7: Passenger Approval and Reservation**

The passenger reviews the quotation and provides approval before authorised staff proceed with flight reservation creation.

**Stage 8–9: Verification and Ticket Issuance**

The booking undergoes verification to ensure that passenger details, flight information, and payment requirements are satisfied before ticket issuance.

**Stage 10: Ticket Delivery and Booking Closure**

The electronic ticket is delivered through an authorised communication channel, and the booking record is updated and closed after the required checks.

## 8. Expected Business Benefits

The proposed AI-assisted workflow may provide several benefits to AA Aviation Sdn Bhd.

**Improved Operational Efficiency**

AI can support repetitive administrative activities and help employees process booking requests more systematically.

**Improved Information Consistency**

Structured prompts encourage consistent passenger information extraction and validation.

**Time Savings**

AI-assisted flight searches, fare comparisons, and document preparation may reduce the time required for routine tasks.

**Better Customer Communication**

Structured quotation and ticket delivery processes can support clearer communication with passengers.

**Improved Booking Documentation**

Standardised outputs can make booking information easier to review, track, and organise.

**Human Decision-Making Support**

AI can provide structured information to help employees make informed decisions while retaining responsibility for important approvals.

## 9. Limitations and Human Oversight

Although AI can support flight booking activities, the proposed solution has several limitations.

- AI-generated information may be inaccurate or incomplete.
- Real-time flight availability requires access to authorised airline or GDS systems.
- Fare information can change frequently.
- Passenger information must be handled securely.
- AI cannot independently guarantee successful reservations or ticket issuance.
- Human verification remains necessary for important decisions.

Therefore, authorised employees must review passenger information, confirm flight availability, approve quotations, verify payments, create reservations, and authorise ticket issuance.

The proposed prompts represent a workflow design and do not independently connect to live airline booking systems.

## 10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how AI prompt engineering can be applied to the flight booking process of AA Aviation Sdn Bhd.

Through ten structured prompts, the portfolio presents an organised workflow from initial passenger booking request to electronic ticket delivery and booking closure.

The proposed solution highlights the potential of AI to improve administrative efficiency, information consistency, and customer communication while maintaining human oversight.

Overall, the project demonstrates how structured AI prompts can support business operations and contribute to digital transformation in the aviation industry.
